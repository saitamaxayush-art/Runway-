# Features

- Story landing: six scroll chapters that carve a Rs 10,000 block into rent, bills, a surprise repair, daily spending and goals.
- Dashboard: balance, daily allowance, day 30 forecast, risk, health score, allocation donut, forecast chart, upcoming dues, alerts.
- Simulator: one-time, weekly or subscription decisions with a before and after table and a commit button.
- Plan: income, spending log, commitments, subscriptions.
- Goals: transfers that reduce balance and show whether the daily need fits the allowance.
- Shocks: five unexpected expenses with a risk preview.
- Day control: advance the month one day at a time, or reset to the sample month.
- Legal: Privacy policy and Terms pages.
